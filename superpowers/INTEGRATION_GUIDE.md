## Available Development Workflows

1. **Brainstorming & Design Review**: Refining requirements and user experience before code edits.
2. **Implementation Planning**: Breaking complex features into modular 2-5 minute tasks.
3. **Test-Driven Development (TDD)**: RED-GREEN-REFACTOR execution cycle with healthcare safety checks.
4. **Systematic Debugging**: Empirical log extraction, traceback analysis, and root-cause resolution.
5. **Healthcare Testing**: PII privacy checks, double-booking prevention, atomic SQLite transactions.
